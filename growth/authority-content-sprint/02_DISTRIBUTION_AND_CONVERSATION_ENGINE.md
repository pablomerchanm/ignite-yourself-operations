# IGNITE YOURSELF — DISTRIBUTION & CONVERSATION ENGINE
**Versión propuesta: 20 oct 2026 | Complementa /insights y el PR #226 | ESTADO: PROPUESTO, pendiente de aprobación y pruebas**
**Objetivo comercial:** generar conversaciones cualificadas, propuestas y depósitos de Authority Platform (~USD 3.000), no acumular impresiones o comentarios por sí mismos.

## Tesis
Insights es nuestra biblioteca propia y canal de búsqueda. La distribución llega a personas a través de **situaciones observables de necesidad**. El contenido debe servir de ayuda en la conversación, no sustituirla.

**Sistema:** señal de transición/credibilidad → comentario útil / conexión genuina → intercambio con comprador → recurso relevante → evaluación de necesidad → Authority Platform → propuesta y pago.

## A. Un solo segmento para el primer sprint
**ICP prioritario (HIPÓTESIS):** profesional senior que está creando una consultoría o práctica independiente tras trayectoria corporativa o institucional; inglés, mercados con poder adquisitivo, especialidad consultiva verificable y posibilidad de decidir la compra.

**Trigger observable:** anuncia salida de compañía, nueva práctica/advisory, empieza captación de proyectos, promueve servicios propios, publica reflexión sobre reposicionamiento profesional.

**Compra potencial:** claridad de oferta + sitio independiente donde mostrar expertise verificable y canal de contacto. CUIDADO: si su problema es solo ausencia de demanda y ya tiene buena web, priorizar diagnóstico y no vender un rediseño inapropiado.

**Segmento alternativo (mantener como segundo experimento, no mezclar ahora):** expert witnesses/forensic economists/technical experts cuyo registro está fragmentado y debe ser evaluado por attorneys. El ángulo es “easier professional evaluation”, no “more beautiful site”.

## C. Los cuatro plays de crecimiento

### PLAY 1 — Trigger-based participation (LinkedIn)
Buscar posts en inglés, recientes, de:
- "launching my consulting practice"
- "starting my advisory firm"
- "after 20 years in corporate"
- "going independent as a consultant"
- "expert witness practice" (segundo segmento)

Registro mínimo: fecha, persona, perfil URL, rol, señal observada y URL, relevancia, comentario previo/si lo hubo, interacción, intención comercial, siguiente acción.

**No contactar automáticamente.** Evaluar si la persona tiene prueba de experiencia, presupuesto potencial, decide compra, oferta entendible o problema de presencia.

**Ejemplo de comentario 1, para una transición profesional real (modificar):**
> One interesting part of this transition is that a senior title tells people where you worked, but not always what clients can hire you to do independently. Have you found it easy to explain your new offer to people outside your former industry?

**Ejemplo 2, cuando alguien ya tiene web pero no clientes:**
> If your website is already clear, I'd separate the problem into two questions: are qualified buyers finding you, and does your offer make sense once they arrive? More design probably won't solve the first one.

**Ejemplo de mensaje de conexión solo si la interacción fue positiva y natural:**
> Hi [Name], enjoyed your post about launching [practice]. The point about [specific detail] stayed with me. I'm working on how experienced specialists translate their track records into an independent professional presence. Would enjoy following your next chapter.

No vender en solicitud de conexión. No mandar enlace ni oferta en primera respuesta no solicitada.

### PLAY 2 — Publish one idea, adapt, converse
Elegir **una observación original por semana** que surgió de investigar buyers, por ejemplo:
- "Your former title is not your independent offer."
- "The credibility gap isn't a design problem first; it's an information problem."
- "A personal website doesn't replace referrals. It helps people assess a referral."
- "You might not need a new website. You might need distribution."

Distribución:
1. LinkedIn personal (idea + pregunta real).
2. Juanita (video sencillo con ángulo propio o experiencia genuina, no recitar copia).
3. Ignite página institucional (síntesis discreta y enlazar artículo cuando realmente exista).
4. X (argumento corto, no copia del LinkedIn).
5. Reddit solo cuando un usuario hizo la pregunta (respuesta nueva, no content seeding promocional).
6. Artículo Insights cuando la pregunta merezca tratamiento profundo.

**Evitar CTA de reunión en cada post.** La página/perfil debe dejar claro servicio y contacto; publicar artículo/CTA cuando aporte a la conversación.

**Post LinkedIn para Pablo (nuevo, si valida voz):**
> After twenty years at a company, an executive can have an impressive résumé—and still struggle to answer one simple question: "What should I hire you for now?"
>
> The title tells us what they were responsible for.
> The independent offer has to explain who needs their judgment, at what moment, and why their background is relevant.
>
> That's a positioning exercise before it's a web design exercise.
>
> For people who've gone independent after a corporate career: what took longer to figure out—the offer, or finding your first clients?

**X (nuevo):**
> A corporate title gives your work context. When you go independent, that context disappears for strangers. Before designing a website, answer: who hires you, for which decision, and what evidence supports your expertise?

Prospective partner TYPES, not fabricated people:

### PLAY 4 — Content-supported outbound
Outreach remains highest near-term revenue potential. Use verified content not as mass blast but as optional context in follow-ups, after person signals interest.

**Lead identification:** contact is publicly confirmed, do not invent email; prospecting source and permission, compare Gmail/outreach suppressions. **No automatic send**.

## D. The Conversion Path
1. Prospect sees an authentic insight, reply, referral or outbound.
2. Profile and site immediately explain Ignite and WHO Authority Platform is for.
3. Prospect finds an illustrative article / proof of thinking / portfolio ONLY if approved for publicity.
4. Prospect can reach one real contact method; no mismatch with historical USD 9k/12-week offer.
5. Qualification: What opportunity are you building? Who evaluates your expertise? Where does your evidence live? What is difficult to find? When are you deciding? Is an independent build within budget?
6. If relevant: outline scope, fixed price based on current approved proposal, timeline, deposit (no invented outcomes).
7. Track proposal/payment and source with the lead.

## E. 14-day commercial sprint — proposed
### Day 1: infrastructure
- Confirm live hero/pricing/contact, optimize Pablo/Juanita/IGY profiles, pin one clear service explanation.
- Set up simple lead tracker with source, URL, segment, status, follow-up date and qualified conversation.
### Days 2–5: first signal test
- Find 10 verified transition posts on LinkedIn. Select only 5 for substantive replies (not generic).
- Publish Pablo post about title vs independent offer.
- Juanita records one own-voice 40-second video if available.
- Identify 5 qualified partner types/names with real URLs, invite 2 to talk.
- Contribute one good Reddit answer with no pitch, no link.
### Days 6–10: targeted distribution
- Follow up on people who reply, not everybody.
- Publish one Insight article once technical route and CTA live.
- Share one X idea and reply genuinely to 2 topical threads.
- Send up to 5 tailored, verified partner outreach notes / next step followups; no unverified emails.
- Qualify anyone who expresses interest in owning a professional presence.
### Days 11–14: learning & sales
- Publish second Pablo post (credible records / referral).
- Juanita or Ignite publish second angle without duplicates.
- Review counts of buyer-relevant replies, inbound qualified conversations, meetings, proposals and paid deposits.
- Compare effort per qualified contact across LinkedIn, partners and Reddit/X. Decide KEEP/ADJUST/STOP. Do not infer SEO outcomes from 14 days.

**Daily allocation proposed (in addition to user's direct outreach time):**
- 15 min: find 1–2 exact conversations.
- 15 min: reply thoughtfully and maintain previous threads.
- 15 min: partner/prospect follow-up and log.
- 15 min: reuse research into one content asset / outline.
Scale only if qualified conversations, not generic engagement, grow.

## F. Mini buyer qualification
**Qualified** = relevant senior professional, independent practice/complex record, plausible budget or decision power, visible need/timing, invites a commercial next step.
**Not qualified** = marketer liking post, student asking career advice, random follower, consultant who clearly says site already solves problem, prospect who requested no contact.

## G. Experiment evidence
For each channel log `date | person/post source | URL | why ICP | message actually published | replies | meaningful conversation | call | proposal | deposit | revenue | time spent | next step`.
Do not treat drafts as posts or tool result search listings as warmed leads.
No metric claims before data actually exists.

## H. Decision gates (after 14 days)
- If LinkedIn yields substantive ICP conversations, deepen comment-led discovery and direct relationships.
- If partners respond, produce one useful co-created resource, not an automatic affiliate scheme.
- If Reddit mostly produces peer discussion, keep as research/authority with smaller time cap.
- If X attracts founders/marketers but few paying senior prospects, reduce activity.
- If no qualified conversations anywhere, challenge the offer positioning/ICP and prospect selection before doubling post volume.
- Continue 2h daily direct outbound only if deliverability and manual followup workload remain healthy; prioritize personalized follow-ups to genuine interest.
