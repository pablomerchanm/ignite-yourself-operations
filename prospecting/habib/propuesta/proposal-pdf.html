01 — Why now

A remarkable record, spread across many places.

Dear Professor Habib, thank you for taking the time to look at the preview. Few surgeons can show a record like yours: living-related liver transplantation in Africa in 1993, radiofrequency liver resection published in 2002, the Habib 4X and EndoHPB instruments that went on to AngioDynamics and Boston Scientific, and first-in-human research from adenovirus gene therapy to small activating RNA. Alongside it, 430 journal articles and companies from EMcision to MiNA Therapeutics.

Today, that record is spread across many places, each written for a different reader: Imperial’s profile for academics, the NHS directory for patients, company pages for investors and PubMed for the papers. Some dates don’t match from one source to the next. When a congress organiser, a journalist or a future partner looks you up, that is often the first impression.

What we’re proposing is our Authority Platform: a complete, done-for-you website on your own domain, built on the preview you’ve already seen. Nothing in it is fixed. We adjust the photographs, the texts and the narrative angle with you, until it presents your career the way you feel most comfortable with and the way that is most useful to you.
