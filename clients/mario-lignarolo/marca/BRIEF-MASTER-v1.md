# 0. READ THIS FIRST

This document is the strategic source of truth for the next design/build iteration of **mariolig.com**.

Do not treat this as a generic web-design brief.

The goal is not to build a “personal brand website,” a coaching funnel, or a portfolio template.

The goal is to create a **concise personal authority platform** that lets a visitor understand Mario Lignarolo quickly, feel the coherence between the different parts of his world, see evidence of what he has actually built, and know how to go deeper.

The governing principle is:

> **Do not say what we can demonstrate.**

Do not tell visitors Mario is impressive, visionary, multidisciplinary, entrepreneurial, a thought leader, a founder, a creator, or an authority.

Instead:
- show LIGNAROLO;
- show Hipertrofia del Ser;
- show Psicocibernética;
- show his writing;
- show his thinking;
- show his visual world;
- show real work and real evidence.

Let the visitor form the conclusion.

The second governing principle is:

> **Headlines build perception. Body copy only deepens it.**

A visitor who scrolls quickly and reads only the main headlines should still come away with a clear interpretation of who Mario is.

The site should feel:
- editorial;
- intentional;
- restrained;
- confident;
- contemporary;
- visually strong;
- human;
- sophisticated;
- easy to scan.

It should NOT feel:
- self-congratulatory;
- guru-like;
- over-explained;
- like a coaching landing page;
- like a SaaS template;
- like a startup founder bio;
- like a Linktree;
- like a long biography.

# 1. WHAT THE WEBSITE NEEDS TO DO

mariolig.com has five jobs.

## 1. Position Mario

The visitor should quickly understand that Mario’s core territory is:

> **TRANSFORMACIÓN DE IDENTIDAD**

The website should not reduce him to:
- breathwork;
- fitness;
- entrepreneurship;
- spirituality;
- content creation;
- coaching.

These are layers, contexts, tools, and expressions.

The central conversation is identity transformation.

## 2. Make his different worlds feel coherent

Mario has real intersections across:
- entrepreneurship;
- product;
- marketing;
- fashion;
- fitness;
- spirituality;
- conscious breathing;
- self-concept;
- personal development;
- writing;
- content;
- live experiences.

The site should not apologize for this diversity.

It should make the diversity feel like a coherent worldview.

A useful internal interpretation is:

> Mario has spent years exploring how inner identity affects what a person is able to build, express, act on, and sustain in the external world.

## 3. Demonstrate authority without proclaiming it

Authority should emerge from:
- point of view;
- lived trajectory;
- projects;
- products;
- writing;
- evidence;
- clarity;
- visual confidence.

Avoid language such as:
- “visionary”;
- “thought leader”;
- “world-class”;
- “renowned”;
- “leading expert”;
- “serial entrepreneur”;
- “high-performance founder”;
- “transformational leader”.

Do not invent status.

## 4. Create commercial pathways

Different visitors should naturally find different next steps:

- someone interested in Mario’s thinking → Hipertrofia del Ser;
- someone interested in his methodology → Psicocibernética;
- someone interested in business/fashion/product → LIGNAROLO;
- someone producing a podcast → contact;
- someone organizing an event → speaking / conversations;
- someone who simply wants to follow → YouTube / Instagram / Substack.

The homepage should create these pathways without becoming a menu of links.

## 5. Prepare for Mario’s next chapter

The site should be able to support future:
- speaking;
- podcasts;
- collaborations;
- live experiences;
- workshops;
- events;
- community;
- new products;
- writing;
- media opportunities.

Do not overbuild those systems now.

Just make the architecture ready.

# 18. REFERENCE: NIKE ABOUT

Direct reference:
https://about.nike.com/en/

Use it as a reference for principles, NOT as a visual template.

What is valuable:

## 1. Monumental typography
Nike allows a phrase to become the composition.

For Mario:
use one or two large thesis moments where typography occupies the screen.

Do NOT make every section huge type.

## 2. Rhythm through scale
Nike alternates:
- large typography;
- large imagery;
- smaller editorial copy;
- image grids;
- content modules.

For Mario:
avoid repeating the same container/card structure section after section.

## 3. Photography is the design
The interface often recedes so image and content can carry the page.

For Mario:
let photographs explain his different dimensions.

## 4. Hard edges
Avoid excessive rounded cards and soft UI.

## 5. Authority without self-description
Nike shows work, people, history, collaborations and stories.

For Mario:
show projects and ideas instead of proclaiming status.

## 6. System, not sameness
Different content types can feel different while still belonging to the same brand.

This is critical because:
- LIGNAROLO is product/fashion/business;
- Hipertrofia is editorial;
- Psicocibernética is a method/experience.

They should not be forced into identical cards.
