Is this personal branding?

Not in the manufactured sense. We do not create a persona or turn you into an influencer. We research, organize, articulate and give form to authority that already exists.

I already have a page through my practice or institution.

An Authority Platform does not replace it. It gives your own body of work a clearer home and creates another trusted route into the practice, company or institution around you.

Do I need to become active on social media?

No. The Authority Platform is an owned, permanent asset. Content and reach can be added later, but constant posting is not required for the platform to be useful.

How much time will you need from me?

One 30–45 minute interview, access to the relevant materials, and one focused review round. We handle the research, narrative, design and development.

How long does it take?

Most platforms are completed within 7–10 business days after the Authority Interview and receipt of the required materials. Ignited, the full system, takes 12 weeks.

Will I own the platform?

Yes. The platform, narrative, core copy and digital asset are built around your name and delivered for you to own.

What is the difference between the Authority Platform and Ignited?

The Authority Platform is the owned place for your work, built in one focused production week. Ignited is the complete twelve-week system around it — positioning, narrative, visual universe, profiles, Ignite OS and a launch.
