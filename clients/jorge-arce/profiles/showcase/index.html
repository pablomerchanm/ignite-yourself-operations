How it was built

Four moves. Hers, specifically.

She was asked for nothing but her name, her links and a photograph. Everything else is what the work produced.

01 · Discover

We go looking

Official biographies, national and trade press, institutional pages, company sites, talks and the archive the person holds themselves.

Found in her case: Global Coffee Platform official biography · IDH Sustainable Trade profile · Daily Coffee News · Comunicaffe · Revista Espresso · La Patria · CNCafé · Herencia VL · 2015 archive, Colombia

02 · Structure

We organise it

Into the sections that make sense for that profession. A board chair is not read the way a surgeon is read.

Hers became: the position · standing · trajectory in three layers — the institution, the company, the table · in print · speaking · where she serves

03 · Verify

She confirms it

Every item goes back with its source attached. She keeps it, corrects it, or removes it. No questionnaire, no blank page.

Of 23 items: 15 open to a published source · 8 exist on the page only because she confirmed them · 1 held back pending a donor’s approval

04 · Monitor

It stays current

A new interview, a new appointment, a new stage. We find it, check it, and ask before it goes on. Most professional pages are written once and then quietly go out of date.
