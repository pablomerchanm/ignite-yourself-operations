The position

Stated in her own words

Most projects here don’t fail for lack of money. They fail because someone treated an adaptive problem as a technical one.

A technical problem is solved with more resources. An adaptive problem is solved by changing the choreography between the people involved. Roughly eight in ten projects get that diagnosis wrong — and the cost of the error lands on the farm, not in the room where it was made.

Twelve and a half million coffee-growing families still live in poverty, after years of a growing conversation about sustainability. That distance between the conversation and the farm is the whole problem.

Comunicaffe International, April 2026
