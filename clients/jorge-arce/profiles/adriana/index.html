About

In her own words

I founded Herencia VL in 2017 and I chair the board of the Global Coffee Platform. My work is the design and governance of regenerative value chains in coffee, cocoa and palm, between Latin America and Africa.

I am the fourth generation of a coffee family from Manizales. I live in Haarlem, and I work wherever the chain does.
