Service, without servility.

What thirty-five years taught me about guests, teams and standards, from two conversations recorded in 2026.

01Convictions

In my own words.

  1. 01

    A complaint is a gift.

    “Every time a guest has complained and been not so polite to me, it was a learning: what we had to correct and what we did wrong.”
    Inspire Someone Today, 2026
  2. 02

    Improvement is not spending.

    “The biggest misconception… improvement is spending.”
    HospeTalk, 2026
  3. 03

    Data before instinct.

    “If you’re not working with data… that’s more of a dictatorship.”
    HospeTalk, 2026
  4. 04

    Luxury is what your guest says it is.

    “What luxury means to your customer will come from data.”
    HospeTalk, 2026
  5. 05

    Good to great touches the heart.

    “I was in a business, not in a charity. Good to great really happens when you do something that touches somebody’s heart.”
    Inspire Someone Today, 2026
  6. 06

    Respect yourself first.

    “If you have to respect others, you’ve got to first respect yourself.”
    Inspire Someone Today, 2026
  7. 07

    Hire for attitude.

    “We don’t hire for skill, we hire for attitude.”
    HospeTalk, 2026
  8. 08

    Stay relevant.

    “Reinvention is the name of the game.”
    Inspire Someone Today, 2026
Transcribed from HospeTalk, episode 35, and Inspire Someone Today, episode 180.

The Indian guest today
01

Better travelled.

Today’s Indian traveller is often more travelled than the foreign guest, and expects the real thing: a Mexican dish had better be Mexican.

02

Experiences over brands.

Ready to spend on a unique experience, not just on a name. Indian hotels now compete for the Indian guest.

03

Service needs boundaries.

A more demanding guest means young front-line teams need the confidence, and the backing, to draw a line.
