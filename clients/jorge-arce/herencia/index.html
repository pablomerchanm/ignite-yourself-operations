Kilimanjaro Water & Food for All

Tanzania · 2023 – 2027

Six complementary projects, financed by donors from four countries, consolidated into one landscape programme at the foot of Mt. Kilimanjaro, Tanzania: water, regenerative coffee, native trees and resilient maize seed.

APSColombia in Magdalena & Cesar

Magdalena & Cesar, Colombia · 2020 – 2021

Fedepalma and its partners drove the Colombian Sustainable Palm Oil Programme to promote production, protection and inclusion in the country's palm zones. Eleven palm oil clusters adopted the APSColombia Protocol and strengthened their extension services.

Cacao of Excellence Programme

Global · 2021 – 2025

Most cocoa farmers live below the poverty line in a market worth 8–10 billion USD a year. The Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT scaled the programme to recognize quality and diversity of cocoa at origin, benefiting more than 1.7 million smallholders, organizations and businesses along the value chain.

Mujeres con Futuro

Cartagena, Colombia · 2019 – 2021

In Colombia, nine out of ten adolescent mothers leave school. With the support of LAZ and BMZ, the Juanfe Foundation ran two projects, Orgullo de Mamá and Mujeres con Futuro, to expand its programme in Cartagena: 36 young mothers trained and placed in formal jobs in 2019, 800 more in 2020–2021, and 51 tourism companies trained in social responsibility.

Kilimanjaro Education, Hope and Future

Kilimanjaro, Tanzania · Since 2014

An education programme by African Partnerships Kilimanjaro (APK) for the children of Kilimanjaro’s coffee communities. It combines two projects. Maize for Education supplies maize grown on APK farmlands to 50 rural schools, so students get the meals they need to learn. Kilimanjaro Classrooms of Hope rehabilitates 100 deteriorated, poorly lit classrooms in 17 public primary schools, using safe, non-toxic materials and setting up maintenance systems that last.
