The trouble with link lists

Most bio links are a list of buttons.Everything weighs the same.

People arrive from a post, a talk, an interview or a referral: curious, and short on time. A list of identical buttons hands them a decision you should have made for them.

02No identity

03No proof

04No signal

You rarely know which link worked, or which post brought the sale.

A Hub decides for the visitor. That is the whole point.

Is this a Linktree?

It does the same job: one link that leads everywhere. The difference is that it is designed, written and built around you, with one action leading the page. You don’t assemble it; we do.

Where can I use it?

Anywhere you share one link: your Instagram, TikTok or YouTube bio, the featured section on LinkedIn, your email signature, or a QR code on stage.

Can I edit it myself?

Changes go through Hub Care. Send a message and we update it within two business days, so the design stays intact.

What do you need from me?

Your links, your main offer, the press or credentials you want shown, and good photographs if you have them. We work from what already exists.

Do I need my own domain?

It works best on yours, as yourname.com/links or a subdomain. If you don’t have one, we help you set it up.

Who owns it?

You do. The page is built for you and delivered as an asset you own. Hub Care is optional.
