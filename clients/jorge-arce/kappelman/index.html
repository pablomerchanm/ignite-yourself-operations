A point of view built over decades

Technology problems are rarely only technical.

When complex systems fail, the cause is rarely a single defect or decision.

It is more often found in the interaction of architecture, requirements, project methods, management, contracts, communication, incentives, organizational conditions and people.

Leon’s work brings these dimensions together—so difficult questions can be examined rigorously, explained clearly and acted on responsibly.

The system is only part of the story. The decisions, structures and people around it matter just as much.

One body of knowledge. Applied across different kinds of consequential work.

For counsel

Software disputes demand more than technical fluency.

They require the ability to examine complex evidence, understand the interaction between technology and management, identify the questions that matter and explain those findings clearly to attorneys, judges and juries.

Leon provides independent research, consultation, analysis, reporting and testimony in matters involving software projects, intellectual property, system performance, cybersecurity and information-systems management. His public case experience includes work for both plaintiff and defense.
